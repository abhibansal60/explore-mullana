## 1. Town basics

- Mullana is a village-level settlement run by its own Gram Panchayat, not a municipal committee. [V] Census/LGD data: https://villageinfo.in/haryana/ambala/mulana-st/mullana-139/ (LGD code 57595, census village code 057595). Wikipedia also says "Rural, Panchayat": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mullana
- Ambala district has 7 revenue tehsils: Ambala, Ambala Cantt, Barara, Mullana, Saha, Shahzadpur, Naraingarh; 4 CD blocks: Ambala, Ambala Cantt, Barara, Naraingarh. So Mullana is a (sub-)tehsil, but its CD block is **Barara**. [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambala_district . villageinfo labels it "Mulana St" (sub-tehsil). OSM address: "Mullana, Barara, Ambala, Haryana, 133203".
- Constituency: Mulana (Vidhan Sabha, assembly constituency), Ambala (Lok Sabha). [S] villageinfo link above.
- Population, Census 2011, Mullana village: **4,956** (2,613 M, 2,343 F), 910 households. [S] villageinfo link above.
  - Sex ratio: ~896-897 females per 1,000 males (computed from the above). Wikipedia says 869/1000 and "approx 8,000" population with no source, so **disagrees; prefer the census-derived 4,956 and ~897**. [?]
  - Literacy: 85.15% overall (M 91.41%, F 78.33%); 3,783 literate. 744 Scheduled Caste. Area 650 ha. [S] villageinfo link.
  - The MMDU student/staff population is not in these numbers, so the real daytime population is far higher. [inference]
- Languages: Hindi (official), Haryanvi, Punjabi widely understood (border area). [S] Wikipedia Mullana; Punjabi is an inference, Haryana's 112 service lists Hindi, English, Punjabi, Haryanvi: https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/chandigarh/from-today-dial-112-for-all-emergency-needs-in-haryana-282377
- PIN **133203** (Mullana S.O.), STD code **01731**. [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mullana ; PIN also in villageinfo. Note: MMDU's own website address uses "Mullana-Ambala 133207" in some listings [?] (Careers360 snippet); 133203 is the Mullana S.O. PIN.
- Vehicle registration HR-54 (private), HR-37 (commercial). Elevation 264 m. [S] Wikipedia Mullana.
- Coordinates: **~30.275 N, 77.048 E** (OSM/Nominatim "Mullana, Barara, Ambala"); MMDU main campus **~30.251 N, 77.045 E**. [S] https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org (query "Mullana Haryana", "Maharishi Markandeshwar University Ambala"). **Wikipedia's infobox coordinates (30.1502 N, 77.0242 E) are about 13 km south of the real village; do not use.** [?]
- Nearby: Barara (CD block HQ and municipality, ~4 km; Wikipedia says MMDU is ~3 km from Barara: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barara), Kalpi, Saha, Do Sarka, Mahesh Nagar on the same highway (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambala%E2%80%93Jagadhri_Highway). villageinfo states 6 km to Barara and 32 km to district HQ.
- Road distances from Mullana (OSRM, approx):
  - Barara 4 km; MMDU campus 3 km; Saha 15 km
  - Ambala Cantt station 24 km (~32 min); Ambala City station 33 km. Sources quote "about 32 km Ambala to Mullana" (villageinfo; search snippets), which matches the Ambala City / district HQ figure.
  - Yamunanagar-Jagadhri 28-33 km (OSRM 33 to Yamunanagar centre, 28 to Wikipedia's Jagadhri point); Ambala-Jagadhri highway is 50 km total, passing Mullana (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambala%E2%80%93Jagadhri_Highway)
  - Kurukshetra (Brahma Sarovar) 55 km; Chandigarh 71 km; Panchkula 64 km; Chandigarh Airport 64 km; New Delhi ~229 km.

## 2. History and temples

- Old name **Devi Garh**, "in reference to nearby ancient temples". [S, single Wikipedia sentence, no citation] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mullana . Origin of the current name "Mullana": [GAP]. Wikipedia's "named for" field lists Mata Bala Sundri Mandir, but this reads like a list of local businesses, not an etymology. [?]
- Mata Bala Sundri Mandir, Mullana: exists, has a trust (Devi Mata Bala Sundri Mandir Trust, Mullana-Ambala; Facebook page only, which I did not use for content). **No reliable non-social source found for its history, legend, deity form details, or mela dates.** [GAP] The deity Bala Sundari is a form of Tripura Sundari/Durga; temples of the same name in Kashipur (Chaiti Mela, Chaitra Navratri) and Trilokpur/Sirmaur have documented melas (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maa_Balsundari), but these are different temples and must not be attributed to Mullana. Ask the temple trust or local priest for the Navratri programme; do not assume.
- Barara, 4 km away, is known for a very large Dussehra Ravana effigy (Wikipedia: "world's tallest", 210 ft in 2016). [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barara . Worth a "Today" listing on Dussehra (20 Oct 2026), but confirm each year.
- Other temples, gurdwaras, heritage in Mullana itself: [GAP]. Haryana Tourism pages were not fetchable (certificate error).

## 3. MMDU (Maharishi Markandeshwar (Deemed to be University), Mullana)

- Founded **1993** as MM Engineering College; deemed-to-be university status **2007** (the 2007 date is from Careers360, [S]). Founder: **Tarsem Garg** (founder Chancellor). [V] https://www.mmumullana.org/about-us (site structured data lists founder) and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharishi_Markandeshwar_University,_Mullana

Campus: 14 hostels, 1,500-seat auditorium, gyms, pool. [S] Wikipedia link above.
- NAAC grade: **sources disagree.** MMDU's own site metadata says "accredited by NAAC as Grade 'A'" (https://www.mmumullana.org/about-us); a Careers360 search result says "A++". [?] Use "NAAC-accredited" until the university's accreditation page is checked.
- NIRF 2024 (Wikipedia, [S]): engineering band 151-200, medical 35, pharmacy 26. Treat as dated.
- Student numbers: [GAP] (no official figure found).
- Official site: https://www.mmumullana.org
- Admission/enquiry (official site header): **+91-1731-274475, 274476, 274477, 274478; toll free 1800 2740 240**. [V] https://www.mmumullana.org/about-us
- Hospital emergency number: [GAP]. Not on pages I could read. Use 108/112 in the guide, and link the hospital site (mmimsr.mmumullana.org) for its own number.

## 4. Getting there

- Highway: the **Ambala-Jagadhri Highway** (national highway-category road, NHAI, 50 km) runs Ambala Cantt - Mahesh Nagar - Saha - Kalpi - Mullana - Do Sarka - Jagadhri. HSRTC buses run it regularly. [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambala%E2%80%93Jagadhri_Highway . The NH number is not stated there; I did not verify "NH-344" for this exact stretch. [?]
- Railway: no Mullana station found. Nearest is **Barara (RAA)**, ~4 km, on the Ambala-Yamunanagar-Saharanpur line; Barara is connected to Ambala, Jagadhri, Saharanpur, Ludhiana, Delhi (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barara; code from https://www.railyatri.in/stations/barara-raa). Main junction **Ambala Cantt (UMB)**, ~24 km (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambala_Cantonment_Junction_railway_station). Others: Ambala City (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambala_City_railway_station), Yamunanagar-Jagadhri (YJUD, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamunanagar-Jagadhri_railway_station), Jagadhri Workshop (JUDW). "Dhulkot" was not verified and is probably unrelated.
- Airport: Chandigarh International Airport ~64 km road (OSRM).
- Buses: Wikipedia says Barara has buses to Yamunanagar, Chandigarh, Kurukshetra. Specific Haryana Roadways route numbers and timings: [GAP].
- Auto / shared transport: [GAP] (no source; ask local owners).

## 8. Places within about 30-75 km (driving distance from Mullana, OSRM)

Coordinates are from Wikipedia where it has them, else OSM.

- Sadhaura, Yamunanagar: 27 km. Historic town (Sadhaura Sahib, Sikh history is commonly cited but not verified here). 30.416 N, 77.225 E (OSM).
- Jagadhri / Yamunanagar: 28-33 km. Brass and plywood city, district HQ. 30.168 N, 77.301 E. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagadhri
- Adi Badri, Yamunanagar: 43 km. Source of Saraswati river tradition, ancient temple site near the Shivalik foothills. 30.458 N, 77.341 E (OSM). Description is general knowledge; not source-checked.
- Kapal Mochan, Bilaspur: 45 km. Hindu-Sikh pilgrimage pond with Mahadev temple and gurdwara; fair in November. 30.326 N, 77.318 E. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapal_Mochan
- Kurukshetra Brahma Sarovar: 55 km. Large sacred tank, Gita Mahotsav venue. 29.96 N, 76.83 E. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahma_Sarovar
- Sheikh Chilli's Tomb, Thanesar: 53 km. Mughal-era tomb. 29.977 N, 76.828 E. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Chilli's_Tomb
- Panchkula: 64 km, 30.698 N, 76.855 E. Mansa Devi temple (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mata_Mansa_Devi_Mandir).
- Pinjore (Yadavindra Gardens): 74 km. Mughal-style garden. 30.793 N, 76.913 E. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yadavindra_Gardens
- Morni Hills: 69 km. Hill area and wildlife sanctuary above Panchkula. 30.705 N, 76.954 E (OSM).
- Kalesar National Park: 70 km. Sal forest in the Shivalik foothills. 30.367 N, 77.533 E. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalesar_National_Park
- Gurdwara Panjokhra Sahib, Ambala: named on the Ambala district page (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambala_district); coordinate and distance not found. "Gurdwara Manji Sahib Ambala" could not be located in any source I could read. [GAP]

Only Sadhaura, Jagadhri and Barara fall inside 30 km; the rest are 43-75 km, so label them as day trips.
